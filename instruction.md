# Project Update – Birthday Surprise for Wasthu

Build a personal romantic birthday website for my boyfriend, Wasthu.

## Main Concept

This website should feel like a personal digital diary created by his girlfriend. It must look natural, handmade, emotional and genuine.

Do NOT make it look AI-generated, like a commercial birthday template, or like an overly designed romantic website.

## Website Sections

### 1. Opening Birthday Wish
- Display "Happy Birthday Wasthu ❤️"
- Add a simple opening animation.
- Show a Sinhala-English mixed birthday message.
- Include a button: "Open my little surprise".
- Make the message feel like a real personal letter.

### 2. His Childhood
- I will upload his childhood photographs.
- Display them in a natural photo album / scrapbook layout.
- Add small handwritten-style captions.
- Do not generate or replace photographs.
- Preserve the original photographs.

### 3. Our Love Story (2019–2026)
- Create a timeline from 2019 to 2026.
- Each year should have its own section.
- I will provide our real photos, memories and descriptions.
- Use my actual information only.
- Do not invent relationship events or memories.
- Include both happy moments and difficult times in a respectful way.
- Tell the story naturally, like I am personally telling him.

### 4. Letters for You
- Create personal letter cards.
- Use simple English and Sinhala-English mixed writing.
- Keep the writing warm and realistic.

### 5. My Voice
- Include a voice-message player.
- I will upload my own recorded voice message.
- Make the audio player simple and easy to use.

### 6. Our Future
- Show a small Sri Lanka-to-Japan distance connection.
- Include a message about wanting to be together soon.
- Keep it emotional but simple.

## Design Rules

- Warm cream, beige, soft pink and natural brown.
- Use paper textures, photo frames and scrapbook details.
- Handwritten-style headings with readable body text.
- Keep animations slow and subtle.
- Avoid excessive hearts, glitter, glowing effects and complicated transitions.
- Avoid generic romantic quotes.
- Do not make every section look like a separate AI-generated card.
- Use realistic spacing and a consistent design.
- Make it mobile-friendly because he may open it on his phone.

## Important

This project must feel like something I personally created for him.

Never invent our memories, dates, conversations or personal experiences. Use placeholders until I provide the real content.

The most important thing is that he feels loved, remembered and appreciated when he opens this website.
